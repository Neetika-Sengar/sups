Sustainability Rating in Fashion Industry

Sustainability Ratings are increasingly used by clothing brands to evaluate their environmental, social, and ethical practices. For example, brands like Patagonia and Eileen Fisher ofthen recieve a high rating due to their commitment to using recycled materials, ethical labor practices, and transparency in reporting their environmental impact. These ratings clearly show that such brands are committed to reducing their carbon footprint and supporting fair trade practices.

A McKinsey & Company report says that more than 60% of consumers look for the use of sustainable materials before purchasing clothes. Hence, sustainability ratings fo beyond corporate responsibility and majorly influences consumer behavior and brand reputation..
