What is Sustainability Rating?

A Sustainability Rating is a tool through which the environmental, social, and governance (ESG) performance of companies, products, or services can be evaluated. It helps consumers and stakeholders make informed decisions by evaluating the sustainability practices and impact of organizations. These ratings offer a transparent and systematic approach to measuring how sustainable an entity is, based on particular criteria.

The objective behind these ratings is to provide stakeholders with a clear picture of the company, in terms of sustainability performance.
